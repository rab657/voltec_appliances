# Positioning

We are **not** selling on price. We are selling on **which cell goes inside the pack**.

The buyer is an assembler whose own name goes on the finished battery. His customer comes
back to *him* when a bank sags after eighteen months. So the argument is not "our cells are
cheap" — it is **"put a cell in there you'll never have to apologise for."**

Three pillars, in this order:
1. **EVE is a name, not a mystery cell.** Assemblers know it; it needs no defending to the
   end customer.
2. **Grade-A, verifiable.** QR-traceable, full capacity test report in the box. You can prove
   what you bought — and so can your customer.
3. **The cheap cell is the expensive one.** A weak or mismatched cell drags the whole bank
   down; you replace a pack, lose a customer, and pay twice.

Never name a competitor brand. Punch up at the *category* of unbranded / unsorted / no-report
cells, never at a named rival — in these groups the rival's dealer is reading too.

# Video brief — "every cell is tested" (the verification ad)

Shoot **vertical** (phone upright), 1080p+, 20-30s, with audio. Steady — brace the phone or
rest your elbow. Speak plain Roman-Urdu while filming.

Shot order — the proof has to be *watchable*, not claimed:

| # | Shot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **0-3s: meter probes touch a cell, reading appears.** Close, in focus. | The hook. Anyone scrolling stops for a number appearing on a screen. Never open on a logo. |
| 2 | Move to the next cell. Same reading. Then a third. Same. | **This is the whole argument.** Matched voltage across cells IS what Grade-A means. Show 4-5 in a row. |
| 3 | Close-up of the **QR code** on the cell, then the test report / packing list. | Proof it's traceable to a factory batch — the thing grey-market sellers cannot produce. |
| 4 | Pull back to the **stacked cartons**. | Shows real stock, not a drop-ship photo. |
| 5 | 2-3s on the cell in hand, terminals and busbar visible. | Product beauty, last — not first. |

Say something like: *"Har cell check kar ke jaati hai. Dekhein — voltage same, matched cells.
QR code se factory report verify kar sakte hain. Grade-A cell yehi hota hai."*

**Do not say a price on camera** — the whole ad system routes rate to WhatsApp, and a spoken
price can't be edited out later without recutting.

Post-production (Claude can do): crop/pad to 1080x1350, burn in Roman-Urdu captions (most
people watch muted, and the voltage-matching point is invisible without them), thumbnail from
the frame where a reading is clearest.
