# Voltec — backlink playbook (2026-08-06)

**Why links matter here:** Google treats links from real, relevant sites as votes of
authority. stabilizer.pk outranks on domain-name + age; our content and schema are
already better. A small number of REAL links closes the authority gap — this niche is
so link-poor that 5–10 good ones is a war chest.

**The rule: never buy links.** No Fiverr packages, no "guest post networks", no PBNs —
that's how sites get penalised out of the results entirely. Everything below is a link
that exists because something true happened.

---

## P1 — The manufacturer link (highest value, we hold the paperwork)
EVE's authorised distributors get public listings (Avnet, Nastech, EEL all do). We are
appointed and documented. **Ask EVE / the appointing upstream to list Voltec** on their
distributor/where-to-buy page with a link to voltecappliances.com.
> Draft (email/WeChat to the EVE contact):
> "We're proud to represent EVE in Pakistan. Could Voltec Appliances be added to your
> authorised distributor / where-to-buy listing for Pakistan, linking to
> https://voltecappliances.com? Happy to provide our appointment reference. This also
> helps us fight counterfeit EVE cells in this market — see our verification guide:
> https://voltecappliances.com/blog/genuine-eve-cells-check-pakistan"
One link from evebattery.com ends the "authorized distributor of eve cells in
pakistan" argument permanently — for Google AND for AI answer engines.

## P2 — Off Grid Guide (customer + YouTuber, door already open)
Bilal Ahmad bought 136 cells from us (invoice, Feb). His channel reviews exactly this.

can buy.

## P3 — Nexcell (customer + partner)
They buy our cells; we retail their packs. A mutual link is the most natural thing on
the internet: their site "cells supplied by Voltec Appliances" → us; our /industrial or
partner row → them. Ask when confirming their `bought` entry.

## P5 — The press story (real consumer-protection angle)
"Counterfeit lithium cells are flooding Pakistan's solar boom — here's how to check
yours" is a genuinely newsworthy pitch, and our QR/B-mark guide is the substance.
Targets: ProPakistani, TechJuice, Dawn Aurora, solar-industry newsletters.
> Pitch draft (email):
> "Pakistan's solar rush has a counterfeiting problem: rejected Grade-B and fake
> LiFePO4 cells sold as new. We published a free verification method (QR decode,
> B-mark, safety-valve check) that any buyer can run in 10 minutes:
> [guide link]. Happy to provide photos of fakes, test-bench footage, and comment as
> a Lahore manufacturer since 1995. — Raheel Ahmad, Voltec Appliances"

## P6 — Forums, honestly done
DIY Solar Forum + PakWheels + solar Facebook groups already discuss fake EVE cells.
Answer real threads helpfully and link the guide where it genuinely answers the
question. Never drop links without substance — one good answer beats ten spams.

---

## What NOT to spend on
- Paid "DA 50+ guest posts" — poison.
- Reciprocal link farms and web directories nobody visits (except the P4 citation set,
  which exists for NAP, not authority).
- Widget/badge schemes.

## Measurement
Links show up indirectly in `scripts/rank-report.py` positions (the scoreboard is the
outcome). Direct check: Search Console → Links report (manual, in the GSC UI) shows
who links to us — worth a look monthly.
